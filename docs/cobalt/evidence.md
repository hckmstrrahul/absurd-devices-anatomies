# Cobalt: evidence

Cobalt is an original design, not a tracing. The renders in `docs/devices/presentation/` (ref-1 to ref-4) set the genre and the finish to aim for; nothing in them was measured or copied. Every position comes from `web/src/cobalt/layout.ts`, in millimetres, with the origin at the board centre (x right, z toward the viewer).

Hand check: at the default 40 kS/s the module packs 40 000 / 16 384 = 2.441 frames a second, draws 180 + 9.2 × 40 = 548 mW and settles at 25 + 548 × 0.038 = 45.8 °C. The test `matches the hand check at 40 kS/s` asserts these numbers. A second one: U1's first front lead is 9.5 pitches (4.75 mm) left of the package centre, on the body's front edge.
